Should Love Landscape help singles meet?

Love Landscape maps how you relate — attachment, ambiguity, closeness, conflict — onto a personal 3D terrain. People keep asking us the same question: could you introduce me to someone whose landscape fits mine?

Honest answer: nothing like that exists yet, and it may never. We're deciding whether to build it, and the only signal that matters is whether people actually want it. Joining this list is just that — a signal. No matching, no profiles, no promises.

We'll use your email for exactly one thing: telling you what we decide (and giving you first access if we build it). No sharing, no marketing. Reply to any email — or write to us — and we'll delete it.

Haven't seen your landscape yet? Take the assessment — it's free, takes about five minutes, and nothing here requires it.
