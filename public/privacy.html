Privacy

Last updated July 2026 · back to the app

The one-paragraph version

Your questionnaire answers never leave your device — the terrain is computed and rendered locally. What we store is your result code (the 13 computed parameters), anonymous usage counters, an optional email-only account, and purchase records. We sell nothing to anyone, share nothing with third parties, and you can delete everything yourself.

Your free-text notes to the AI are sent to the language model to generate your reading and are not stored by us. AI readings use the provider configured in Settings (the managed service by default). Research contribution is a separate, explicit opt-in and is stored anonymously, disconnected from accounts and from everything above.

Sharing and its limits — read this before publishing

  • A published share page is visible to anyone with the link. It shows your terrain and its code — never your answers or notes.
  • Unpublishing removes the page, but messaging apps may keep cached preview images for a while.
  • Your landscape code itself encodes your 13 parameters. Once someone has copied a code, they can decode it forever — unpublishing cannot take codes back. Share codes the way you'd share the information in them.
  • If someone shares their code with you and you save the comparison, that code is stored with your data (see table above). Extend them the same care.

Deleting your data

Without an account: everything lives in your browser — clearing site data removes it. Results synced for features (saving, sharing, purchases) can be deleted from My Landscapes.

With an account: My Landscapes → “Delete my account and data” removes your account, landscapes, comparisons, and purchased readings immediately. Anonymous counters are irreversibly disconnected from you. Purchase records keep only what payment reconciliation requires (no reading content). Devices you used may hold local copies until their site data is cleared.

Who processes data

  • Supabase — database and authentication hosting
  • Vercel — application hosting
  • Stripe — payments (we never see your card number)
  • Resend — delivers sign-in code emails
  • Your chosen AI provider — receives parameters (and notes, if given) to write readings; the managed default routes via OpenRouter

No advertising, no analytics vendors, no data brokers, no sale of data — ever.
