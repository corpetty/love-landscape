## 2. Defensibility of archetypes-over-dimensions

The scientific position that survives adversarial review:

- **Measure dimensionally, communicate categorically — with the continuous profile always
  visible.** This is precisely what attachment research does: taxometric analyses (Fraley
  et al. 2015, ~4,700 adults, CCFI values well below the .45 dimensionality threshold)
  show attachment is continuous, yet the field still communicates via styles as *regions
  of a continuous space* [verified]. Love Landscape's architecture already matches this:
  13 continuous params are the measurement; archetypes are the communication layer.
- **What sinks MBTI is not having types — it's claiming discrete natural kinds**, forcing
  dichotomous cuts on unimodal distributions, and instability at cut points (Stein & Swan
  2019; bimodality-artifact literature — sources fetched). Mitigations already built or
  cheap: nearest-prototype (no hard cut lines), margin + runner-up ("an Archipelago with
  Estuary edges"), and copy that never claims the types are kinds.
- Person-centered methods (latent profile analysis) are legitimate in modern psychology
  (Annual Review source fetched) — the long-term defense is to *re-derive the prototypes
  empirically* via LPA on contributed parameter vectors and show the editorial archetypes
  approximate empirical profiles.

## 3. The strongest adversarial critiques (and what to do)

1. **1–2 items per construct is far below reliability norms.** [verified] The brief NFCS
   uses 3 items per facet selected from a 41-item parent instrument (N=1,584 validation);
   even 7–10 item facets only reach α ≈ .62–.82. A 19-item / 13-dimension instrument
   cannot honestly claim measurement-grade reliability.
   *Mitigation:* honesty copy ("a structured reflection tool, not a psychometric
   instrument"); report 2-item dimensions with Spearman–Brown (source fetched); grow
   items in the paid Full Reading flow where depth is welcome.
2. **P12 conflates anxiety and avoidance.** [verified] The ECR-R's two dimensions behave
   differently; q16's own options already distinguish them (panic-pursue = anxious,
   withdraw = avoidant) but the scoring collapses both to "low security."
   *Mitigation:* tag attachment *style* from the chosen option, not just a scalar.
3. **P8 conflates self-reflection and insight — which have opposite wellbeing
   correlations** (SRIS: reflection correlates with anxiety; insight with flexibility and
   wellbeing) [verified]. Scoring "high self-knowledge" as uniformly adaptive is wrong.
   *Mitigation:* copy audit — frame P8 as "explored," never as "healthy."
4. **P3 conflates initiating vs receiving touch** (CIT scale) [verified]; **P9 conflates
   need / enjoyment / productivity of solitude** (PSS) [verified]. Acceptable
   simplifications for v1 — name them in the methods page.
5. **P5 vs P6 redundancy — empirically survivable.** [verified] Need for closure and
   intolerance of ambiguity correlate only r ≈ .29 (r ≈ .36 for the closest facet), so
   keeping them separate is defensible.
6. **Barnum/Forer effects.** Mitigate with falsifiable, non-universal copy: every
   archetype description should contain at least one statement most people would *not*
   endorse (the "low" notes), and no universally flattering claims.
7. **Unvalidated formula weights.** True and unavoidable pre-data. Mitigation: the code
   is public; label weights as editorial priors; recalibrate from contributed vectors.
8. **ROCI-II irony.** [verified] The standard conflict instrument itself has questioned
   factor structure and no established predictive validity — a useful humility point:
   even "validated" instruments are contested. Cite Gottman's demand-withdraw work as the
   stronger anchor for P10.

## 4. Validation roadmap (using contributed parameter vectors)

1. **Now:** descriptive distributions per dimension and archetype; archetype assignment
   margins (how many users are near boundaries?).
2. **Retakes** (account holders): test-retest stability of params and archetypes;
   benchmark against ECR-R's 85%-shared-variance/3-weeks.
3. **Convergent validity substudy:** invite a volunteer subset to also complete NFCS-15,
   ECR-R short form, SOI-R, SMAP; correlate against the matching Love Landscape
   dimensions. This is the single highest-value study and is cheap to run.
4. **Structure:** EFA on contributed vectors — do 13 dimensions collapse into fewer
   factors? LPA — do empirical profiles resemble the 10 archetypes? Re-fit prototypes.
5. Publish a public methods page ("What this is and isn't") naming anchor constructs,
   citing instruments, and stating limitations. The methods page *is* the defense.
