The structural point is taken (external advisors + frozen public thresholds added), but the conclusion — that unenforceable gates invalidate the plan — would rule out all solo-founder experimentation. The document's job is to make the kill criteria unambiguous enough that self-deception is effortful; it cannot supply willpower, and pretending otherwise would be its own dishonesty.

The phases are now clearly labeled as future decisions, not approved plans.
