# Round 3 — Adversary

1. **Gate metric I is arithmetically unreachable at the gate's own pass thresholds.** 25,000 completions × 25% singles × 20% waitlist = 1,250 waitlisted singles nationally; I simultaneously requires ≥2,000 in one metro — impossible even at 100% geographic concentration, and Phase 0's channels have no concentration mechanism. Branch A requires 3–10× overshoot. Either the outcome distribution is Branch B/C by construction, or the numbers get rewritten at the freeze — gutting the pre-commitment story.

2. **The gate numbers are set after the experiment starts.** Freeze happens against "Step 0 + one month of instrumented data" — post-registration: the founder observes in-experiment trend, then sets the bars, having sunk Step 0 plus a month. The memo requirement binds only post-freeze changes; the threshold-setting step itself — the most gameable moment — is unprotected.

3. **Dispositions neither exhaustive nor consistent.** (a) A kills + C/E/H/I pass → no branch fires. (b) H=18% middle simultaneously resolves-to-pass (nearer boundary) and triggers Branch B ("H middle with A–F passing") depending on rule order. (c) "Nearer boundary" undefined for categorical metrics B and I. (d) "Middle-with-passing-trend" undefined for point-in-time metrics.

4. **"~90% no-regret" is circular and quantified by assertion.** Do-nothing is rejected because it forfeits improvements "every other path wants" — but no-regret is *defined* as wanted-under-every-alternative, excluding the path that falsifies it. No per-requirement effort mapping: under B2B licensing, F0.3/F0.6/F0.7 are growth-loop investments; by effort the dating-and-virality-specific share is plausibly 30–40%, not 10%. The figure is load-bearing in §1, §3.3, §6.0, §7 and never derived.

5. **"No-regret" conflates strategy-invariance with outcome-invariance.** Branch C (maintenance mode, <12,500 users) is the regret world where the ~200 hours were mostly wasted — and finding 1 suggests it's likely. Honest claim: cost shared across strategies *conditional on continuing*. Also: §6.0's opportunity-cost set excludes everything outside Love Landscape.

6. **A cheaper same-question experiment is missing from §6.0:** a standalone fake-door intent test (landing page + waitlist to the blog audience, days, ~$500) before the 4-month build. Its absence exposes the bundling: packaging uncontroversial improvements with the experiment makes approving pivot infrastructure feel like routine maintenance. Also absent: licensing the instrument to an existing dating app; selling/open-sourcing.

7. **H and I measure channel × seed audience, not the product — and the gate's metrics are anti-correlated by design.** The blog audience is heavily partnered; a kill on I may mean "the blog audience is partnered," a pass may be a seed quirk. The strongest sharing mechanic (partner comparison) recruits non-singles, so success on C structurally suppresses I. The table treats nine metrics as independent when its two most important are coupled against each other; nothing in §8/§9 accounts for it.

8. **The Phase 1 gate is unachievable under the document's own resourcing.** +9 months requires 75K new assessments (2.5× acceleration), 100 paying couples with cycle-2 renewal observed, an AI guide shipped, 5,000-in-metro waitlist (2.5× an already-unreachable bar), and a fielded pre-registered validation study — one person, nights and weekends, no funding event anywhere between Phase 0 and Phase 2. Either an unstated hiring assumption or a gate designed to fail, collapsing "a pass buys the right to spend more."

9. **The Step 0 intent baseline is a biased proxy treated as calibration-grade.** The "single/exploring fraction" comes from an optional relationship-*structure* question, post-results, answered only by research volunteers — importing exactly the selection/framing biases F0.8 was designed to eliminate. The document flags stated-intent inflation everywhere except the dataset it proposes to calibrate against.

10. **"It is the swipe format — not online dating — that is declining" is a causal claim the data can't carry.** Equally explained by Match's capital reallocation to Hinge, brand-freshness cycles, cohort effects; "depth-positioned" is a category constructed after seeing which apps grew (Hinge is prompts-and-likes, not psychometric depth). §3.1 bounds the entrant inference but leaves the format-causation claim — which motivates §3.2's design thesis — unbounded.
