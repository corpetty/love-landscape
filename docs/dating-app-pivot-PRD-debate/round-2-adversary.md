# Round 2 — Adversary

1. **Kill criteria advertised as "pre-committed" but explicitly not.** §1/§8 sell "numeric, pre-committed, published"; §9 admits thresholds are provisional, finalized after month-1 baseline. You cannot pre-commit to numbers you reserve the right to reset after seeing early data. Approve now and the actual bar is set later, unilaterally, by the person with the strongest incentive to pass it.

2. **"Kill" outcomes are not kills — every failure branch pre-authorizes continued work.** Virality failure → app remains free tool + SKU; intent failure with virality success → "continue as assessment/couples business (Phase 1)." A failed Phase 0 gate still routes into Phase 1. When every branch leads to "keep building," the gates constrain nothing; the request is really "bless whatever I do next."

3. **The zombie zone.** Gate ≥25% share, kill <12.5%; gate ≥3% paying, kill <1%. The most likely outcome is the ambiguous middle (18% share, 2% paying) and the plan pre-commits to nothing there. Escalation of commitment — the actual mechanism by which cheap Phase 0s become expensive Phase 2s — is never addressed.

4. **The timeline is also an escape hatch.** "Deadline moves, not the bar" — adjudicated by the founder. Advisors are unnamed, founder-selected, hold no veto. "The pivot dies in four months" is unenforceable.

5. **Phase 0 cannot produce the evidence it claims to price.** F0.8 is a costless checkbox at the emotional peak of a fun quiz result; stated hypothetical intent is notoriously inflated, and the doc itself prices waitlist→active as low as 20% in Phase 2 kill criteria (2,000 waitlisted ≈ ~400 actives). A passed intent gate demonstrates nothing about liquidity; the unprecedented step remains untested until Phase 2. Honest framing: "Phase 0 tests virality plus a soft survey."

6. **The founder hasn't looked at his own data before asking for approval.** §2 admits baseline must be pulled; the research flow already captures relationship status ("Single / Exploring" in ResearchContribution.jsx) flowing to an existing admin dashboard. A weekend of querying existing data could kill or reshape the thesis for free. "Cheapest way to find out is Phase 0" is a false dilemma — cheaper probes exist and were not compared.

7. **"Near-zero cash" hides the real cost: 200+ founder-hours.** No alternative use of the four months is evaluated (couples-only product, B2B for therapists/coaches, the blog). The PRD compares Phase 0 against nothing, and anything beats nothing.

8. **Conflating two decisions to launder the risky one.** Accounts + share pages + premium SKU needs no PRD; the only contested content is the dating pivot, which is "not approvable today." What is actually requested is installation of the dating pivot as the default trajectory while deferring every falsifiable commitment. Rename it "assessment growth experiment" and the pivot loses presumptive status — which is precisely why the document doesn't.

9. **Systematic confession-as-inoculation.** §3: market data doesn't support the pivot. §4: no precedent. §5: no moat. §7: success doesn't fund the next step. Every evidentiary section returns "no," and the recommendation is still "go," surviving only on "it's cheap" — which per #6–7 isn't even the cheapest option. Candor as load-bearing rhetoric; the conclusion predates the evidence.

10. **Survivorship bias in virality comps; the playbook is era-dependent; no channel math.** 16personalities/5LL/WNRS are three survivors of tens of thousands of dead quizzes; base rate never stated. 16personalities compounded SEO in the open-web era — zero-click search and LLM answer engines have gutted that channel, and F0.6 de-scopes content to 13 pages. 25,000 completions at 70% completion needs ~36,000 starts in 16 weeks from zero with no paid acquisition — from what source? The gate is either fantastical (guaranteeing a renegotiated miss per #1) or decorative.

11. **Internal timing impossibility.** F1.1 is a $59.99/yr sub launching month 4–5; the Phase 1 gate at +9 months requires "cycle-2 renewal ≥60%" — observable at month 17, not 9. A gate metric that cannot exist at evaluation time says the scoreboard was written for persuasive effect.

12. **Fatigue-to-depth chain asserted, admitted broken, left broken.** The Keeper rebuttal self-refutes ("a promise we cannot make"); the tension is deferred to "Phase 0/2 data" but Phase 0 takers do the quiz as entertainment with no dating promise attached — nothing in Phase 0 tests whether dating-motivated users will complete a psychometric intake.

13. **The "why now" collapses under its own competitor analysis.** Funded entrants have an 18–24 month head start; §8 admits a pivot by any of them likely kills the dating layer; the plan schedules its own dating layer for month 9+ contingent on a raise. If the window is real, the plan concedes it by construction; if not, "waiting doesn't make the wedge cheaper" is manufactured urgency (waiting on an unoccupied wedge is free). A fatal-if-realized risk is graded "Medium."

14. **Even total success dead-ends at an unexaminable funding assumption.** Best case yields $600–900K ARR that doesn't fund the $500K–1M Phase 2 build; the sole raise evidence is one comp. If the option Phase 0 prices can almost never be exercised, the expected value of Phase 0 *as a dating experiment* rounds to zero — information with no decision it can inform.

15. **Undefined denominators make the gate-critical metric gameable.** "≥20% of single completers" — self-reported at results time, post-flattery, with "Single / Exploring" as an identity-flattering option; if the funnel skews coupled (which §5.3 admits), 20% of a small flattered remnant is a weak signal wearing a precise number. No minimum singles base rate specified.

16. **The defensibility story is circular.** Durable assets = unvalidated instrument + pair dataset that starts at zero (research data firewalled) + community that doesn't exist. The moat exists if everything works; everything working presumes the moat. Why does an unmoated wedge in a contracting category deserve even the option premium?

17. **Approval is sequenced before the diligence that could invalidate it.** Payment flow unverified, zero tests, deployed state unknown — yet approval is requested for a growth experiment whose gates depend on that stack. F0.0 + baseline audit costs days and could invalidate every number in §9. Approve-then-verify is backwards; the only beneficiary of the ordering is the narrative.
